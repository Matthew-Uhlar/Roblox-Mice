# World, story & lore (v0.1)

> Brainstorm action item: "World/Story/Lore". The tone is silly and warm, aimed
> at all ages. Every joke should work for a 9-year-old and still amuse their
> older sibling.

## The premise: the Great Cheese Heist

The world of **Fromagia** runs on cheese. Its continents are made of different
cheeses: Swiss highlands, Parmesan beaches, Cheddar volcanoes, Mozzarella moons.
For a thousand years the **Herd**, an order of elephant guardians, protected the
cheese and lived peacefully beside the mice, who were welcome to the crumbs.

Then the **Mouse King**, Squeakeasy the Grate, discovered the **Rind Gate**: a
web of portals that connects every cheese-land to every other. He swore the mice
would never eat crumbs again, opened every portal at once and sent the
**Nibble Horde** to carry off all the cheese in Fromagia.

The Herd answers. Elephants are famously afraid of mice, so every elephant who
steps through a portal is very brave or very hungry (usually both).

## Why the game plays the way it does

| Mechanic | In-world reason |
|---|---|
| Any map can follow any map | The Rind Gate links every land, so your herd picks which portal to charge through (route cards). |
| Bosses | Each land's cheese is guarded by a champion the Mouse King has hired, bribed or (in Grandma's case) annoyed. |
| Curses | The Mouse King's wizards hex the portals. A strong hex follows you through every gate. |
| Blessings | Grateful locals reward the herd after a boss falls. |
| Double or Nothing | The **Cheese Bookie**, a suspicious rat in a visor, offers to double your haul if you dare one more portal. (Earned cheese only, never Robux.) |
| Go Home | At the end of a cycle the Rind Gate briefly points back to the Herd's sanctuary, and your haul is safe. |
| Cycles | Every cycle pushes deeper into the Rind Gate, where the cheese is older and stronger, and so are the mice. |
| Bots | Herd cadets, enthusiastic trainees who follow you anywhere (labelled 🤖). |

## The Herd (classes)

- **Elephante**: retired heavyweight stomping champion who still owns the belt.
- **Ele Fonte**: fountain-keeper from the Plains and a water mage. Speaks only in proverbs about hydration.
- **Ellie Fawn**: forest ranger raised by deer. Throws peanuts with alarming accuracy.
- **Le F'ante**: fencing master from a Brie-country chateau. *En garde, petit rongeur!*
- **Filet**: the Herd's chef, who insists on seasoning every mouse before throwing it out.
- **Jumbo**: an elephant whose ears are big enough to fly. Doesn't like to talk about it.
- **Fizzante**: soda alchemist. Everything they brew fizzes, and most of it explodes.

| Ancient Ice Dragon | Has guarded the Blue Cheese Tundra so long it has forgotten why. |
| Void Cat | The only thing the mice fear. The Mouse King summoned it anyway. Regrets were had. |
| Speedy Gorgonzola | The fastest mouse alive, so smelly you can see him coming. |

## The final arc (post-launch)

Cycle depth reveals the **Mouse King's Rind Palace**, a later content beat. The
twist: the mice were hungry because the Herd hoarded the cheese. The true ending
is a shared feast, which sets up a "Mice & Elephants together" co-op season.
